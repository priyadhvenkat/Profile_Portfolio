Projects

UCF Capstone · Team project with an industry partner

Server Telemetry Anomaly Detection with TCN Autoencoder

Built a deep learning model that learns the normal behavior of servers from telemetry time series and flags unusual workloads using reconstruction error. Simple statistical methods couldn't separate the workloads well, so the team moved to a Temporal Convolutional Network Autoencoder (TCN-AE).

  • My role: data preparation (finding gaps and timestamp issues, normalization) and IQR-based anomaly detection; worked with the team on model building, validation, MLflow tracking, the GitHub repo, and the Streamlit app
  • Result: reconstruction loss stayed around 0.25% on normal data but jumped to 3.77% on the CPU stress test, about 15× higher. PCA, t-SNE, and KL divergence confirmed the same ranking.

Machine learning · Forecasting

Supply Chain Inventory Loss Prediction

Used 2018–2023 inventory audit data from a medical device company to predict and reduce write-off losses from sales reps' inventory.

  • Cleaned messy labels (for example, about 15 spellings of one audit type), handled nulls, and standardized risk ratings
  • Compared 9 regression models, with Random Forest performing best; built classification models for risk rating and grouped reps into Best, Better, and Poor performers
  • Forecast future write-offs with Prophet, which showed a rising trend with a year-end peak
  • Recommendations: targeted training before peak season, tighter audits, and mentoring by top reps

Los Angeles Crime Analysis Dashboard

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard on 2.1 million+ LA crime records (2010–2019) to show when, where, and what types of crime happen, and who is affected.

  • Cleaned the data with Python and Power Query, and wrote DAX measures for KPIs like crime rate per 100,000 people
  • Found that crime peaks in summer, evenings, and weekends, and that 77th Street and Southwest are the highest-crime areas
