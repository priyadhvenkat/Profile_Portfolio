About Me

I'm a data scientist and data engineer based in Florida, with an MS in Data Analytics from the University of Central Florida. I spent over five years at Tata Consultancy Services on NielsenIQ's retail data systems, first as a database developer writing Oracle PL/SQL and tuning PostgreSQL, then as a technical lead building Azure data warehouse pipelines, data quality checks, and Power BI reports.

At UCF, I focused on machine learning and deep learning, including a team capstone that used a temporal convolutional autoencoder (TCN-AE) to detect anomalies in telemetry data. I like work that connects reliable data engineering with useful models and clear dashboards.

Outside of work, I enjoy portrait drawing and painting.
